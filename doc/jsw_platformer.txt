NON-NEGOTIABLE GAMEPLAY RULES
=============================

The player movement must follow the GDD direction.

Movement:

* Walk left and right.
* Fixed jump arc.
* No mid-air steering on a plain jump — once the player jumps, the jump
  direction and arc are committed and horizontal velocity is locked until landing.
* Exception: while the jetpack equipment is actively thrusting, mid-air
  horizontal steering is allowed (that is the jetpack's whole purpose). This
  is the only case where air control is permitted; it must not leak into a
  plain unequipped/non-thrusting jump.
* Once the player jumps, the jump direction and arc are committed.
* Landing instantly stops horizontal movement.
* Gravity should feel constant and old-school, not modern and floaty.
* Death on contact with spikes, enemies, lasers, crushers, or other lethal hazards.
* The room resets on death.

Approximate movement target:

* Walk: steady grid-readable movement.
* Jump: about 1.5 tiles high and about 3 tiles wide.
* No acceleration-heavy modern platformer feel.
* No coyote time unless explicitly used as an accessibility option.
* No variable jump height unless implemented as a later optional mode, not the default.

Reference controller shape:

```gdscript
if is_on_floor() and jump_pressed:
    velocity.y = -JUMP_SPEED
    velocity.x = facing_dir * MOVE_SPEED
    jumping = true

if jumping and is_on_floor():
    jumping = false
    velocity.x = 0
```

Controls:

* Left / Right: move
* Space or A: jump
* Down or E: interact / collect / trigger console
* Escape: pause

Optional controller support is welcome, but keyboard must work first.

==================================================
ROOM STRUCTURE
==============

Space Miner uses fixed single-screen rooms.

DECISION (supersedes earlier "hand-authored .tscn per room" assumption):
Rooms are generated procedurally via scripts/tools/AsteroidCampaignGenerator.gd
and RoomLayoutGenerator.gd, not authored as individual Room_001_*.tscn scenes.
This is the accepted approach going forward — do not migrate to hand-authored
per-room scenes. Any work on rooms should extend/tune the generator (layout
rules, hazard placement, exit rules, deterministic seeding) rather than
building the JSW-style Room_001_LandingBayAlpha.tscn file layout described
below. Landing Bay Alpha and other "named" rooms should be produced as a
fixed/seeded output of the generator, not a bespoke scene.

The rules below still apply to whatever the generator produces per room —
they constrain the generator's output, not the authoring method:

* No side-scrolling camera.
* Each room is a complete puzzle box.
* Each room loads instantly.
* Exits are at screen edges, pressure doors, shafts, lifts, or sealed doors.
* Player spawn position must depend on which exit was used to enter the room.
* Room solutions must be deterministic (same seed/room ID -> same layout, every run).
* Every room must be solvable without RNG affecting solvability (a fixed seed is fine;
  the generator must not roll different, potentially-unsolvable layouts on replay).
* Hazards and enemies must use repeatable timing patterns.

Target full game:

* 50+ interconnected rooms (the generator already targets 56 — that meets this bar
  once layouts are validated for solvability and readability).

Practical finish-first target:
Use the generator to produce a curated, fixed-seed release-candidate route first,
rather than hand-building rooms:

* Landing Bay Alpha
* Control Hub
* Refinery / Workshop / Oxygen area
* Key or power-up gated branch
* Core Access
* Fusion Chamber / ending room

Minimum acceptable finish target:

* 10 to 15 polished, playable rooms (curated seeds/IDs from the generator, verified solvable)
* complete start-to-ending route
* at least 3 room themes
* at least 3 enemy or hazard types
* at least 2 power-ups or progression items
* working oxygen, score, pickups, room reset, and ending

After the playable route works, expand validation/tuning toward the full generated map.
